Moves

Each turn: play a card onto one of your routes (ascending, backing cards first) or discard it, then draw from the deck or take the top of a discard pile (not the one you just discarded to). A started route scores (sum − 20) × (1 + backing cards), +20 with 8 or more cards. The game ends when the deck runs out. A green dot marks cards you can play; outlined opponent cards are ones they took from a discard pile. Ventures is an original game on a classic mechanical skeleton, an independent implementation.
