PPO self-play beats the heuristic

Four cumulative training runs of a pure reactive policy (no search) on Ventures. Each run adds one change to the one before it. The first-version PPO stalled near 1340 Elo; the final recipe clears the hand-tuned heuristic and beats plain PIMC search, an opponent it never trained against.
